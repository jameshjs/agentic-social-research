Build a civic technology product that helps people understand, navigate, or respond to a real urban problem facing Toronto in the 2026 municipal election.

Teams should identify a concrete problem faced by residents, journalists, community organizations, policymakers, or other civic users, and build a useful prototype in response.

Projects should use Toronto urban data as a foundation, but remain open in approach. The goal is not to predict the election or support a candidate, but to make civic information more useful, accessible, and actionable.
