Papiers: Autoresearch Challenge

AI agents can now read papers, write code, run experiments, and autonomously explore ideas. But most autoresearch systems operate independently of the humans behind them.
What if agents could build on what you've previously read, questioned, and learned? What if research teams could combine the knowledge and judgment of multiple people? And what if agents could keep humans informed, ask for expertise, and help them learn from new discoveries?
We want you to explore what research could look like when humans and AI agents work together.
